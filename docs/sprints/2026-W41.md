## What we learned

- **Index checks stopped on 29 September.** Spend has been $0 every day
  since; the last check ran at 00:22 UTC on 28 September. The DataForSEO
  account balance is below zero: the trial credit is spent, so every run
  is refused and pauses for the day, while the cron still answers 200.
  Nothing has opened or closed a post page for twelve days.
- **The queue is over its cap.** 481 queued against `QUEUE_MAX` 420: the
  refusal probably comes while collecting in-flight tasks, before the
  trim runs. To be confirmed in the code.
- **Stories run at three a day, not five.** 41 jobs queued, 21 published,
  15 rejected as stale: a third of the queue ages out unwritten. Story
  views went from 3 to 18.
- **Topic pages carry the site.** Tag views rose from 31 to 82 and are now
  the most-viewed page type; `/tag/movies/` brings a third of all search
  clicks. Feed views fell from 73 to 51.
- **The reader still converts nobody.** 9 reader views in two weeks, every
  `reader_start` empty, 0 follows and 0 posts read.
- **429s on submission are gone** (3 → 0) with no change to the limit;
  422s rose from 3 to 5. Preview → success held at 82–93%.
- **The SEO check measures escaped HTML.** `scripts/seo-check.mjs`
  takes the description straight from the markup, so quotation marks and
  ampersands count five or six times over; the four "long" story
  descriptions are 155–157 characters. A check that fails on correct
  pages teaches people to ignore it.

## What changes

- Top up DataForSEO, or decide to run without index checks for now.
  Until then no post page opens, and the "pages in Google" number cannot
  move.
- An account refusal should be loud and visible in the cron log: today
  a stopped run and a working one both print `HTTP 200`.
- Trim the index-check queue before anything that can stop the run.
- Decode entities in `scripts/seo-check.mjs` before measuring titles and
  descriptions, so `npm run seo` is clean again without touching pages.
- Fewer story jobs a day, or a writer that keeps up: queueing five and
  publishing three wastes the clustering.
- Keep a fifth of the sprint for tech debt and learning; it slipped
  entirely this time.
