# Sprint 2026-W40 (29 September – 5 October)

The plan, written at the start of the week; the retro on 6 October adds
what was done and the numbers.

## Goal

Stories every day, and topics that are about their topic.

v1.1 started on 28–29 September: story jobs, a worker API, review and
publishing ([#20](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/pull/20),
[#21](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/pull/21)); the first five
stories went live on 29 September, written by hand through the job API.
The review and critique of the same day found topic pages and their
exports dominated by a few busy feeds, and duplicate topics competing for
the same query — the pages that carry the head keyword.

## Scope, in order

1. [#28](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/28) Queue story jobs
   every day — story, S. Stale and repeatedly abandoned jobs leave the queue.
2. [#23](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/23) A topic's feed
   follows the topic, not its busiest feed — story, M. Today 25 of 30 posts
   in `/tag/ai/rss.xml` are from dev.to.
3. [#24](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/24) One page per
   topic — story, S–M. `/tag/ai/` and `/tag/artificial-intelligence/` are
   both indexed.
4. [#25](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/25) Keep a reader's
   followed feeds on iPhone — story, M.
5. [#26](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/26) Cloaking test
   that tolerates moving pages; one helper for reader links — tech-debt, S.
6. [#27](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/27) Submissions that
   hit 429, and the three failing feeds — learning, S.

Items 5 and 6 are the week's fifth for tech debt and learning.

## Blocked on the maintainer

- [#29](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/29) and
  [#30](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/issues/30), the automatic
  writer and its fact check: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, the daily `llm` budget
  (proposed: $3), and the go-ahead on keeping prompts, source weights and
  editorial exclusions in a private repository. Until then the queue is
  worked by hand, and every story waits in review for an admin.
- The DataForSEO cap (W39 retro): $0.12 a day buys about 40 checks, and
  the queue is now held at 420 posts
  ([#18](https://github.com/dstengine/rss-mobi/pull/18)).
